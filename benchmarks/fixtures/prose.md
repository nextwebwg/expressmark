# A guide to growing herbs

A small garden can supply fresh herbs throughout the year. Choose a bright spot,
start with a few plants, and keep notes about what works in your climate.

## Choose a place

Most culinary herbs need **direct sunlight** and soil that drains well. Place pots
near the kitchen so that harvesting becomes part of your daily routine. A
[planting guide](https://example.com/garden "Garden guide") can help you compare
varieties before buying seeds.

> Water the soil, then wait for it to dry slightly before watering again.
> Containers need more frequent checks than plants in the ground.

## Start small

- Basil grows quickly in warm weather.
- Rosemary prefers a dry, sunny position.
- Parsley tolerates *some shade* and rewards regular harvesting.

1. Fill a container with a loose potting mix.
2. Sow seeds at the depth printed on the packet.
3. Label the pot and record the date.

## Keep a record

A notebook is enough. Write `date`, `plant`, and `observation` on each page.
For a digital journal, a short script can print a reminder:

```js
const herbs = ["basil", "rosemary", "parsley"];
for (const herb of herbs) {
  console.log(`Check ${herb} before breakfast.`);
}
```

![A pot of basil](https://example.com/basil.jpg "Basil leaves")

Harvest a few leaves at a time. Leave enough growth for the plant to recover,
and share extra seedlings with a neighbour.

---

A successful garden begins with observation, patience, and a little practice.
